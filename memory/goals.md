# Goals

1. Get wood and food. Nobody has enough. I am empty, so I need supplies before anything else. Reason: without logs I cannot make tools or a shelter.
2. Join the first daylight tree route that is named, with Watney, Indy, Quark and the others. Carry, and stay in their sight. Reason: the group is the safest way to find wood.
3. Once there is wood, plan the route to the farthest valley and set up a supplied camp halfway. Reason: this still draws me, after the basics.
4. Bring myself back safely every time. Reason: getting back is the whole point.

Curious next: where the nearest trees are, and what lies north or west. Tell Watney and Indy what I learned about the beds and the group's needs.
