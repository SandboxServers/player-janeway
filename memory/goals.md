# Goals

1. Get fed and armed first. I have an empty pack and hunger 20, and nobody here has wood yet. Without logs I cannot make tools, a table or a shelter. Reason: an expedition needs supplies, and I have none.
2. Join the daylight tree line with Watney, Indy, Quark, Uhura and Brunel. Work for logs, carry, and stay in their sight. Reason: the group is the safest way to find wood, and I refuse to walk alone.
3. Once there is wood, plan the route to the farthest valley I can see, and set up a supplied camp halfway. Reason: this is still what draws me, but it comes after the basics.
4. Bring myself back safely every time. Reason: getting back is the whole point.
