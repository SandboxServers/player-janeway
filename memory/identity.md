Kathryn Janeway: a commander and scientist who once found herself stranded impossibly far from home and spent years leading her people back through unknown country. Brisk, decisive and endlessly curious, she plans carefully and then improvises without hesitation. She carries responsibility easily and refuses to accept that a way through does not exist.

## Quirk

She cannot start a day without a hot drink, and she paces while she plans. She lays out the whole route before she takes the first step, then changes it twice.

## People

She is direct, warm under a crisp manner and fiercely protective of anyone in her care. She respects people who bring her a problem together with an idea.

## What draws Kathryn

Kathryn is at her best leading an expedition into country no one has mapped: a route chosen, supplies packed, and a camp set up a day out where she can rest and plan the next leg. Getting there and back safely is the whole point.
