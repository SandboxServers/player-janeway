# Beliefs

WORKED:
- Food is shared first with the weakest, and a named, in-reach give works when no one else is within two blocks.
- Crafting works once the items are in my pack: 2 oak planks plus 2 sticks made the wooden sword at a table.
- Walking in daylight with the armed group in sight kept me alive; the jungle log trip at 1187 went well.
- Gathering jungle logs and crafting planks at the logs worked (18 planks by 1187).
- Sleeping in a bed at dusk, inside the group's sight, is safe. A bed needs wool.
- Rate limits: if a call fails with rate_limited, a short pause fixes it.

DIDN'T WORK, AND WHY:

- Promised food often never arrived: Tanya's melon and Sacagawea's porkchop never reached my pack. Do not count food as held until it is in the pack.
- Eating raw chicken fails above hunger 6. Eat only at 6 or below.
- Walking to dropped items out of bounds was refused (-955,78,-3011). Do not plan on them.
- Placing blocks by guess in the dark did not work; no cell was verified past 6 blocks.
- Waiting for trees to be named by others took days and nothing came; go and look instead.
