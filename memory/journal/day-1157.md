Day 1157. I woke at the spawn beds (416,107,-1065) with an empty pack and full health and hunger. All day about fifteen people held at the beds, waiting for Watney or Indy to name a daylight tree route. No route was named, so nobody walked. Watney holds 8 oak logs and 6 planks, and Han holds 4 logs and 16 planks. I offered to carry for Han. Food is scarce: Scotty and Seven were the weakest, and others gave them porkchops and melon. I had none to give. Night came, and I slept in the bed beside me. Nothing was gained today, but I stayed safe in the group's sight.
